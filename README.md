# Coach House HCD

The studio practice that every Coach House tool is built against.

This repo is the canonical source for how we work — the principles we hold, the process we follow per feature, the patterns we reuse across projects, the audits we publish quarterly, and the scaffolding we drop into every new repo on day one.

It exists because "human-centered design" is one of the most over-claimed phrases in tech marketing. We wanted a version we could defend by pointing at receipts rather than vibes.

---

## Who this is for

- **Coach House team and collaborators** — drop this scaffolding into any new project; refer back when a decision feels wobbly.
- **Clients and partners** — read the principles and the most recent audit; you'll know what to expect.
- **Other small studios building mission-driven software** — copy what's useful. The whole thing is MIT-licensed.

---

## The 7 mechanisms

Coach House HCD shows up as seven concrete things, not seven slogans:

1. **A single canonical practice doc** — this repo, referenced by every Coach House product page.
2. **A project kickoff template** — `PROJECT-KICKOFF.md`. Every new tool starts by filling out a one-pager: who's the worker, what's the rage moment, the dignity test, the measurable claim.
3. **Per-project HCD scaffolding** — `scaffold/` drops `customer-conversations/`, a PR template, and an audit cadence into any Coach House repo.
4. **A cross-project pattern library** — `patterns/` documents reusable design + code patterns shipped across projects.
5. **Quarterly portfolio audits** — every Coach House project graded against the framework in `audits/`. Published as a combined report.
6. **A visible studio signature** — an HCD badge on every product page that links here and to that product's most recent audit.
7. **Public output** — newsletter, white papers, conference talks. Calibrated for sustained pace, not bursts.

## The closing test

If the workers on the other end of any Coach House product read everything in this repo and everything in that product, would they recognize themselves and their work — or would they recognize a vendor's idea of what their work is like?

If the first — we're doing it.
If the second — rewrite.

## Status

This is a **practice in progress**, not a finished framework. Coach House committed to it on 2026-05-20. Year-one targets:

- 12 newsletter pieces (one per month)
- 4 quarterly portfolio audits
- 1 conference talk submission
- 1 additional white paper
- Scaffolding refined from use across 5+ projects

The most recent audits live in [`audits/`](./audits/). What we got wrong is logged there. That's the credibility move no incumbent will match.
