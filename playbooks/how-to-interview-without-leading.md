# How to interview without leading

The shortest version of [`customer-conversations/PROTOCOL.md`](../customer-conversations/PROTOCOL.md). When you have a conversation booked and want to avoid the most common research mistake — confirmation-seeking — keep this open on a second screen.

---

## The one rule

> Ask **how it actually went last week**, not **what they think about a feature**.

Past tense + specific event + their experience = real signal.
Future tense + hypothetical + their opinion = polite noise.

---

## The four phases (one screen)

### Phase 1 — Warm-up (5 min)

**Use this:** *"Walk me through what your job actually involves on a Tuesday. Not what your title says — what you actually spent yesterday doing."*

**Avoid:** *"How are you?"*, *"What are your biggest pain points?"*, *"What do you think of [the product]?"*

### Phase 2 — Workflow walk-through (15 min)

Pick one workflow. Ask them to walk you through how it went last week.

**Use this:** *"Show me how you handled [specific thing] last week."*
**Use this:** *"What did you do next?"*
**Use this:** *"Where did you go to find that?"*

**Watch for:** rage moments (sighs, apologies), workarounds (clever compensations), cross-tool jumps (count them), cognitive load (*"I just have to remember…"*).

### Phase 3 — Forced tradeoffs (5 min)

Preference questions, framed as tradeoffs only.

**Use this:** *"If you could only have one of X or Y, which?"*
**Use this:** *"If you had to give up one thing about your current process to fix one other thing, what would the trade be?"*

**Banned:**
- ❌ *"Wouldn't it be useful if you could do X?"*
- ❌ *"Do you like [feature]?"*
- ❌ *"Would you pay for [thing]?"*

### Phase 4 — Close (5 min)

**The respect close:** *"What would have made this conversation more valuable for you?"*

**The follow-up close:** *"Can I come back in six to eight weeks to show you what we did with this?"*

---

## Within 10 minutes after

Fill in the session note. Write the one-line commit: *"Based on this conversation, we WILL X. We WILL NOT Y."* Calendar the six-week follow-up.

Skipping this step is how research becomes theater.

---

## If you only remember three things

1. **Past tense, not future.** "Show me how it went," not "Would you like…"
2. **One workflow, walked.** Not a survey of opinions.
3. **One commit, written.** Before the conversation feels like it ended.
