# Project Kickoff — East Broadway Business Association

> **Filled retroactively on 2026-05-20.** EBBA web app launched before the kickoff template existed; this captures the answers as they would have been written on day one, reconstructed from the live product and the project memory.

## Project name

**East Broadway Business** — a directory + map + events + leadership + get-involved site for the East Broadway Business Association in Louisville.

Live: https://east-broadway-business.vercel.app
56 verified East Broadway businesses, co-located ones grouped on the Mapbox map, with leadership info and active forms.

---

## Who's the worker?

Two roles, both worth naming:

**Primary user — the neighbor planning something on East Broadway.** Often a Louisville resident or visitor trying to figure out which businesses are on the strip, where they are, what hours they keep, and which to support. They open the site on a phone, often shortly before deciding where to go for dinner / coffee / shopping on a Friday or Saturday.

**Secondary user — the EBBA board / leadership.** Volunteer leaders maintaining the directory, posting events, fielding "get involved" form submissions, surfacing neighborhood programming. They are not full-time professionals; the tool has to be light enough to maintain alongside the rest of their day jobs.

**Buyer:** The EBBA leadership (volunteer board); some funders may eventually attach to the corridor's programming. The buyer's win is *more visibility for East Broadway businesses* and *easier inbound for new members + events.*

---

## What's the rage moment?

> *"I want to take the kids out on Friday but I don't know which East Broadway places are family-friendly past 6pm. There's no list."*

The lived-out version: someone heard about a "new East Broadway place" but can't remember the name, can't find it on a map alongside its neighbors, and can't see whether it's open Saturday. The friction kills the casual support visit.

Secondary rage moment (leadership side): *"We want to add the new bakery to the directory but the only way to do that today is to edit my personal Google Doc and hope I don't lose it."*

---

## What's the dignity test?

The businesses listed are small. Some have one or two employees. The directory must read like a *neighbor showing off the strip* — not like a vendor cataloging accounts.

❌ Failing version: *"Browse our comprehensive database of merchants."*
✅ Passing version: *"56 businesses on East Broadway. Here's where they are."*

The leadership-facing parts (admin / get-involved fields) must not make a volunteer feel like she's doing a real job in a corporate tool. Light, conversational, fast.

---

## What's the measurable claim?

**Worker-level (neighbor):** A neighbor opening the site on a Friday afternoon finds three businesses she didn't know existed and clicks through to at least one within two minutes.

**Leadership-level:** Adding a new business listing takes a volunteer leader under five minutes from start to publish, without leaving the admin interface.

**Corridor-level:** Get-involved form submissions trend up quarter-over-quarter (target TBD by EBBA leadership).

**How we'll measure:**

- Page-view + click-through analytics on directory entries (already wired via standard analytics).
- Form submission counts.
- Qualitative check-ins with leadership 6-8 weeks after launch.

---

## What are we explicitly NOT solving?

- **East Broadway Business is not a transaction platform.** We do not take orders, bookings, or payments. We connect neighbors to businesses.
- **East Broadway Business is not a yelp-style review site.** Reviews are not the core artifact; presence + location + leadership are.
- **East Broadway Business is not a city-wide directory.** Corridor-specific. The narrow scope is the value.
- **East Broadway Business is not a Resolve KY clone.** The ecosystem section ties to the 20 Resolve KY member orgs because they overlap geographically, but EBBA is its own thing.
